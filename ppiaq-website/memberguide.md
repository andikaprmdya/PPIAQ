# Member Account Guide (Website User)

This guide is for people who use a member account on the website.
It explains daily usage in simple steps.

## 0. Registration and Approval Flow

Before login:

1. Register account first from `https://ppiaqueensland.org/auth/register`.
2. Your first status is **Pending**.
3. You will get status email updates:
   - Pending
   - Approved
   - Rejected
4. You can only login after status is **Approved**.

## 1. Member Login

Login steps:

1. Open: `https://ppiaqueensland.org/auth/login`
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click Sign In.
4. You will be redirected to Community Board.

## 2. Main Member Pages

After login, the two main pages are:

- Community Board: `https://ppiaqueensland.org/community-board`
- Profile: `https://ppiaqueensland.org/profile`

## 3. Community Board (For Members)

In Community Board, you can:

- See partner discount list and discount codes
- See resources and vendor information
- Read announcements and updates
- Subscribe to newsletter with your email

How to subscribe newsletter:

1. Scroll to the newsletter section.
2. Enter your email.
3. Click **Subscribe**.
4. Wait for success message.

## 4. Profile Page and Membership Card

Open Profile to see:

- Membership card
- Member ID
- Membership type
- University and major
- Branch and domicile/campus
- Valid from and valid until dates

You can also see:

- Personal information
- Educational information
- Membership status panel

## 5. Membership Status and Countdown

In Profile, status can show:

- Approved Active
- Approved Non-Active
- Pending
- Rejected

If status is approved, you will see a live countdown:

- If active: how much time left until expiry
- If expired: how long since expiry

## 6. Language Switch (EN/ID)

Use the language switcher in the top header:

- `EN` for English
- `ID` for Indonesian

The interface content will switch language automatically.

## 7. Logout

To log out:

1. Click **Logout** in the header.
2. You will return to login/public page.

## 8. Quick Troubleshooting

### I cannot login
- Re-check email and password.
- Make sure Caps Lock is off.
- If your status is pending/rejected, login is blocked until status changes.

### My membership is non-active
- Your membership period has expired.
- Contact admin to ask about renewal process.

### My profile data is wrong
- Contact admin to update your member data.
