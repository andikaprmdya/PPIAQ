# Election Quick Guide
Admin & voter instructions | PPIAQ Presidential Election

## FOR ADMINS
1. Open https://ppiaqueensland.org/auth/login with an ADMIN account, then go to https://ppiaqueensland.org/admin/elections. Open an existing draft or click + Create draft.
2. Add or edit the title, introduction and opening/closing times (Brisbane / AEST, UTC+10). Click + Add candidate for at least two candidates; edit their names and profiles, or use Remove candidate. Click Save draft. Only drafts can be edited or deleted with Delete draft.
3. Verify membership payments, then Tag eligible active Ordinary and Associate members. Check for duplicate people and correct email addresses. The membership ID becomes the Voter ID; the system creates a unique ID if needed. Save draft.
4. Edit the invitation subject and message. Keep {{voterId}} and {{votingLink}} in the message. Click Preview email to check it; this does not send an email.
5. Save draft, tick the financial eligibility / duplicate check confirmation, then click Publish & send invitations and confirm. This locks candidates and the voter list, and sends a separate personal invitation to each tagged member. Voting follows the scheduled opening time.
6. Click Refresh to check delivery and turnout. Use Resume / retry failed invitations for failed or queued messages, or Resend invitation for an unvoted member. Voting ends at the deadline or with Close voting now. Then view Final results and Download results CSV. Cancel election stops voting and retains records.

## FOR VOTERS
1. Open your personal invitation email (check Spam / Promotions) and click Open ballot. No login is needed. Your Voter ID alone cannot open the ballot. Vote within the Brisbane times shown.
2. Read the candidate profiles and select one candidate, or Abstain if available. Click Review choice. Use Go back to change it, or Yes, submit vote to confirm.
3. Wait for “Thank you. Your vote is recorded.” Each invitation allows one vote; submitted votes cannot be changed. If the connection fails, click Check my voting status or reopen the original email link before trying again.

## PRIVACY & HELP
Keep your voting link private: anyone holding it can use it. App admins see who voted, but cannot see your individual choice. Candidate totals appear after voting ends. This is not cryptographic anonymity from database/server operators.
Need help? Email qld@ppi-australia.org with your name and membership ID; never forward your secret voting link.
