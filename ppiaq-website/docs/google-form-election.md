# Public 2026 election form

`/2026election` embeds the original Google Form. Question edits appear on the
website after a refresh, and submissions are stored in that form's Responses.
No website deployment is needed for question edits in Google Forms.

- Editor: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1nT91pL9klbovjA8i5vypIKKxLDo-XzktrN4PsK39JEo/edit
- Responder: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfLYRGehHPtTOmimjV7IFRcQuByqYq5upmIMXQI-f5HvV5K8A/viewform
- Published and accepting responses; responder access is Anyone with the link.
- Google sign-in is optional: verified email collection and Limit to 1 response
  are disabled so signed-out visitors can complete the embedded form.
- The original required CV upload in the self-nomination section is now
  `Tautan resume / CV`, a required short answer with Text → URL validation.
  Applicants must give the committee permission to view the linked document.

Keep file-upload questions out of this form: Google requires sign-in for uploads
and shows a “Fill out form” link instead of the questions in the embed. After
changing access or question types, check `/2026election` in a signed-out browser.
